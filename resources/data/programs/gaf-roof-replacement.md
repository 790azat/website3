## GAF Roof Replacement: The Full Guide to Contractors and Costs

A GAF roof replacement generally starts with a roof inspection to determine the condition of the existing roofing system. The contractor can then establish whether the project requires repairs or a complete replacement.

### GAF Roof Replacement Cost

GAF reports that the average U.S. roof replacement cost was **$17,631 in 2025**, although actual prices vary based on roof size, pitch, location, labor, materials, and project complexity.

A detailed estimate can include:

- Existing roof removal
- New roofing materials
- Installation labor
- Flashing and ventilation
- Roof-deck repairs
- Cleanup and disposal
- Permits and other project costs

GAF recommends getting an itemized estimate so homeowners can identify the costs included in the project and understand potential additional charges.

## Finding GAF Roofing Contractors

Homeowners comparing **roofing companies** should evaluate more than the brand of roofing material being installed.

GAF has several certification levels, including GAF Certified™, Certified Plus™, and Master Elite®. Certain enhanced warranty options are available only through qualifying certified contractors.

When comparing roofing companies, consider:

- GAF certification
- Licensing and insurance
- Written estimates
- Local experience
- References and reviews
- Installation warranties
- Project schedule
- Payment terms

A contractor's GAF certification does not mean the contractor is an employee or agent of GAF; certified contractors are independent businesses.

## Roof Inspections and Emergency Repairs

A roof inspection can help determine whether a damaged roof needs repair or replacement.

After severe weather, emergency roofing work may be necessary to control active leaks or protect the structure while a permanent repair or replacement is planned.

Emergency work can include temporary protection, leak mitigation, and assessment of storm damage before permanent repairs or replacement are scheduled.

## GAF Roof Installation

A roof replacement involves more than installing the visible roofing material. Depending on the project, the roofing system can include underlayment, leak barriers, flashing, ventilation, starter products, and other components.

Before work begins, the contractor should explain what is included and how unexpected conditions—such as damaged roof decking discovered after removal—will be handled.

A written estimate should clearly separate the original scope from potential additional work.

## GAF Warranty Coverage

GAF offers several residential warranty programs, including its standard Shingle & Accessory Limited Warranty and enhanced programs such as System Plus, Silver Pledge, and Golden Pledge.

Enhanced warranties have specific eligibility requirements and are available through qualifying GAF-certified contractors.

Homeowners should confirm:

- Which warranty applies
- Which products are covered
- Whether contractor certification is required
- What workmanship coverage is included
- Warranty duration
- Exclusions and limitations
- Registration requirements

GAF states that enhanced warranties require registration, which the certified contractor handles for the homeowner.
